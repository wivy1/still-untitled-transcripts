# Still Untitled lore guide

## 2012-2013: early projects

Adam, Will and Norm start with an unnamed show about making, films and family. Adam calls his workshop "the cave," questioning the idea that a workshop should be an escape from family life. Early advice emphasizes cardboard, salvaged materials and acquiring tools gradually.

The **Bourne burn bag** becomes a recurring replica project, with listeners and the Replica Prop Forum helping identify its contents. By March 2013, the hosts have enough driving-licence measurements but are still checking possible knives. Adam later gives Will and Norm replica bags made by Indy Magnoli. A perforated wastebasket becomes another identification puzzle. Will becomes a father in 2013. 

## 2014-2016: workshops and career changes

Adam's **first-order retrievability** means reaching a tool without moving another one. He applies it to drawers, toolboxes and rolling storage.

Several prop projects recur over these years. Discussions of **Cain**, the robot from *RoboCop 2*, cover a Tippett visit, Chronicle's collectible and Adam's separate casting. Spacesuit projects include Mercury, Apollo, Clavius and *The Martian*, with different collaborators and completion dates.

Will leaves his Tested day job in 2015 and stays on the podcast, then announces his **FOO** VR project in 2016. Adam's original *MythBusters* run ends in 2016. His mother, Karen, begins appearing on the podcast, and Simone Giertz joins Tested. Stories about Adam's sons turn increasingly to travel, driving and independence.

## 2017: touring and new projects

Adam tours **Brain Candy** with Michael Stevens. The episodes cover rehearsals, prop building and life on the road, including a late-year run of 24 cities in 27 days.

Other projects include Magic Wheelchair costumes, plans for a Studio Ghibli gathering, Adam's *Blade Runner 2049* short-film appearance and Michael Giacchino's birthday concert. The **EDC ONE** bag launches in November and starts shipping in December.

Jen Schachter and Laura Kampf join the guest list. Simone describes exhaustion from making *Manick*. Adam's sons turn 18, and one moves to Los Angeles.

## 2018: unfinished builds

Adam works with Brett Foxwell on his Cain casting and resumes the **Martian suit** at Frank Ippolito's shop. It remains unfinished when Adam wears a separate orange **ACES suit** at New York Comic Con.

The Ghibli meetup takes place, and Magic Wheelchair conversations emphasize designing with the children who will wear the costumes. Andy Weir discusses abandoning **Zhek** after about 70,000 words before writing *Artemis*. *MythBusters Jr.* enters production. Norm takes parental leave, and Adam's previously unnamed build is revealed as a *Blade Runner* blimp, reaching his home office in January 2019.

## 2019: books, television and shared builds

**Every Tool's a Hammer** moves through final edits, audiobook recording, publication and touring. 

**Savage Builds** is revealed after months of unnamed production stories. Jen describes it as her first television job. By December, she is living in Oakland and working in San Francisco.

**Project Egress** brings makers together to build an Apollo hatch replica at the Smithsonian. Later episodes return to contributor credits and the build video. **Tested VR** lets viewers watch makers at their benches, and TwitchCon prompts discussion of streaming. Will announces **Brad and Will Made a Tech Pod** in September.

Films and television keep inspiring builds, including a *Watchmen* Looking Glass mask and a *Rounders* poker-chip carrier.

## 2020-January 2021: lockdown and the final episodes

Adam mourns his dog **Huxley**, discusses his **Spot** robot loan and visits Wellington again. Mark Rober and Johnny Fraser-Allen make their first appearances.

Lockdown brings remote recording, home projects and audience Q&As. Adam's lathe accident interrupts his workshop time. Karen stays with his family; mask sewing, puzzles, baking and delayed Ecto-1 parts recur in conversation. Fatigue and difficulty concentrating become frequent subjects. After George Floyd's death, the hosts discuss racism, parenting, listening and supporting Black makers.
