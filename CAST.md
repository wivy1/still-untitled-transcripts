# Still Untitled cast guide

## Hosts

### Adam Savage

Co-host and main storyteller. A film-effects model maker whose credits include the first two *Star Wars* prequels, *Galaxy Quest* and the *Matrix* sequels, he began co-hosting *MythBusters* with Jamie Hyneman in 2003. Earlier work included assisting sculptor Chico MacMurtrie and teaching model making and materials science at San Francisco's Academy of Art College.

First appearance: [E001 (2012-06-05)](001%20-%20On%20the%20Subject%20of%20Man%20Caves%20-%202012-06-05.md). Last appearance: [E393 (2021-01-15)](393%20-%20All%20Good%20Things%E2%80%A6%20-%202021-01-15.md).

### Norman Chan

Co-host and Tested co-founder. He worked at *Maximum PC* before starting Tested with Will Smith in 2010. He started interning at *PC Gamer* at 15.

First appearance: [E001 (2012-06-05)](001%20-%20On%20the%20Subject%20of%20Man%20Caves%20-%202012-06-05.md). Last appearance: [E393 (2021-01-15)](393%20-%20All%20Good%20Things%E2%80%A6%20-%202021-01-15.md).

### Will Smith

Co-host and Tested co-founder with Norman Chan (2010). Previously a *Maximum PC* editor and podcaster, he studied biochemistry, worked in IT, and wrote for *Ars Technica* before joining *Maximum PC* in 2000.

First appearance: [E001 (2012-06-05)](001%20-%20On%20the%20Subject%20of%20Man%20Caves%20-%202012-06-05.md). Last appearance: [E371 (2020-06-30)](371%20-%20Children%20of%20Makers%20-%202020-06-30.md).

## Recurring guests

Effects artist, Thingergy founder and Tested cosplay collaborator. Before his debut, he had appeared twice on *Face Off*; his builds included Zoidberg, Admiral Ackbar and a Capcom dragon.
